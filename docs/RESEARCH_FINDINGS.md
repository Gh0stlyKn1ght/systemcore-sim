# Research findings

Snapshot: 2026-09-20. Research lead: @Gh0stlyKn1ght.

The expanded [research dossier](research/README.md) replaces the earlier short public-repository summary.

The most important result is **version separation**: early hardware drawings, beta wiring, current OS images, WPILib prereleases, vendor libraries, and competition policy cannot be mixed into one timeless specification. See the [evidence ledger](research/EVIDENCE.md).

A documentation-supported behavioral simulator is a reasonable project direction; exact controller emulation is not the first deliverable. See [the Linux plan](research/LINUX_SIMULATION.md). FIRST itself points teams toward prerelease simulation for transition preparation in its [2026-07-09 team blast](https://info.firstinspires.org/frc-7-9-2026).

The research includes official publications, public repository material, vendor integrations, and annotated Reddit/Chief Delphi discussions. It does not include private beta channels, target-service probing, or independently reproduced hardware benchmarks. The [snapshot](research/SNAPSHOT.md) records what was and was not inspected.
